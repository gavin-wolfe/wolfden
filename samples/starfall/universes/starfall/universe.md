A relay network connects the settled worlds of the Kessan Reach, and one by
one its stations are going silent.

The series *The Long Dark* follows Ilse Tarrow as she finds out why. The
standalone *A Quiet Moon* comes after it in reading order, though it's set
decades earlier, among the people who built the relays.
