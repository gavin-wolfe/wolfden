# Wolfden

**A story bible for writers of long, interconnected fiction.**

Wolfden keeps the lore of your series in one place: characters, settings,
timelines, plot threads, outlines, and every invented thing a world needs
(star systems, factions, ships, spells). Every fact knows where in the story it
becomes true, so you can always ask *"what's canon as of the end of Book 2?"*

> **Status: early.** There's nothing to install yet. You build it from
> source (see [Getting started](../using-wolfden.md#getting-started)).

## Start here

- **[Getting started](../using-wolfden.md#getting-started):** build
  Wolfden, make a den (your Wolfden folder of notes), and add your series
  and books, in order.
- **[Writing in your editor](../using-wolfden.md#using-obsidian):** keep
  writing in Obsidian or [VS Code](../using-wolfden.md#using-vs-code), and
  the [gotchas for other editors](../using-wolfden.md#images-and-other-editors).
- **The web UI:** `wolfden serve` opens your den in the browser, where you
  [make notes](../using-wolfden.md#making-a-new-note),
  [edit them](../using-wolfden.md#editing-notes), make
  [checkpoints](../using-wolfden.md#checkpoints), and
  [freeze a finished book](../using-wolfden.md#freezing-a-finished-book).
- **[Working with AI (MCP)](../mcp-clients.md):** connect Claude or
  another AI assistant to your den.
- **[Packs and types](../types.md):** the kinds of notes Wolfden knows
  about, and how to add your own.
- **FAQ and gotchas:** things that tend to surprise people, in
  [Using Wolfden](../using-wolfden.md#gotchas) and
  [for AI assistants](../mcp-clients.md#7-gotchas-for-ai-assistants).
- **[Where to file things](../where-to-file.md):** whether a note belongs
  to the universe, a series, or a book.
- **[Format specification](../format.md):** the exact rules for the
  files in a den.
