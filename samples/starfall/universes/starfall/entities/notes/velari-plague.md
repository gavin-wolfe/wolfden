# The Velari plague

## The official history
<!-- wolfden: until=ash:5 -->
Every schoolchild in the Reach learns it: a plague killed the Velari sixty
years ago, soon after the first human settlements. It's why their worlds are
empty.

## What really happened
<!-- wolfden: from=ash:5 pin=true -->
There was no plague. The [[relay-network]]'s signal devastated the Velari,
and the plague was the cover story, put about by whoever knew.
