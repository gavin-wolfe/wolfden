# Dates and the calendar

## Dates
<!-- wolfden: pin=true -->
Dates are plain years and months, with no calendar of their own: `2324-03`
sorts by itself. *Ember* runs through 2324 and *Ash* into 2325. *A Quiet
Moon* is set in 2263.

## What's on the timeline
Only what the story needs. The Velari's fall has a rough year in prose, since
nobody in the story can say exactly when it happened.
